Dear UN Security Council President Jérôme Bonnafont and UN General Assembly President Khalilur Rahman,

As Planetary Guardians, scientists, and concerned global citizens, we urge you to have an annual planetary science security briefing.

Seven out of the nine planetary boundaries have been breached — three quarters of our life support system. The recent 2026 Planetary Health Check shows that the boundary transgressions are increasingly severe and headed in the wrong direction. We are seeing the impacts of this around the world.

The health of the planet, human health, economic stability, and security are all deeply interconnected. This has been demonstrated through the suffering caused by the increasing intensity of horrific natural disasters, air pollution, extreme heat, chemical pollution, food and water scarcity, and other symptoms of the breaching of planetary boundaries.

We have the independent planetary science that is critical to help inform your decision making to avert future security, health, and economic risks. We are ready to bring together a group of independent, world class scientists and other experts to prepare and present annual planetary science security briefings.

ASK ONE

Hold an annual Planetary Science Security Briefing.

ASK TWO

Encourage countries to appoint Chief Planetary Scientists.

We also encourage you to ask nations to put in place Chief Planetary Scientists to embed planetary science into decision making to minimise future risks. Belgium has already done this, with several nations set to follow by the end of this year. This community can become a trusted network to identify future security, health and economic risks and to share solutions to get us back to a "safe operating zone" within planetary boundaries.

You can change the course of history for the better by putting planetary science at the core of the UN Security Council and the UN General Assembly.

We would be honored to support you and the citizens you work to protect.

With gratitude for considering this,
Planetary Guardians
